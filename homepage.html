Our Story

At Bread.Co, we believe in simple ingredients, traditional methods, and unforgettable flavor. Every loaf is shaped by hand and baked fresh each morning — no shortcuts, no preservatives, just honest bread made the way it was meant to be.

It started small. A home kitchen, a sourdough starter named Margot, and a stubborn belief that good bread could anchor a community. What began as weekend loaves for neighbors grew into something we never quite expected — a real bakery, a real team, and a real reason to get up at 4am every single day.

Every recipe we use has been tested, tweaked, rested, and tested again. We source our flour from local mills, our seeds from regional farms, and our inspiration from the seasons. When rhubarb comes in, you'll find it in our morning buns. When the days get shorter, the spiced loaves come out. We bake with what's around us.

We're proud to be part of this neighborhood. Come in, say hello, and leave with something warm. That's what we're here for.
